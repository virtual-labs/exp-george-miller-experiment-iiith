Instructions for George Miller's Memory Experiment
  • First, select how many words you would like to remember for this trial. You can start with fewer words and gradually increase the number of words in subsequent trials.
  • Once the words are displayed, focus on memorizing them in the exact order they appear.
  • After the words disappear, you will be prompted to type them back in the same order in the textbox provided. Be as accurate as possible.
  • If you are unsure of the order or forget any words, don't worry! You can try again by selecting a different number of words or retrying the current trial.
  • Once you've entered your responses, click on "Submit" to view your score, which will show how many words you recalled correctly.
  • Keep practicing and try to improve your score as you go. You can repeat the experiment as many times as you like!
